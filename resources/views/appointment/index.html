✔ Your complete recovery roadmap for a successful implant journey.

⏰ The First 24 Hours

Most important actions immediately after surgery.

🩸

Bleeding Control

  • Bite on gauze 30–45 min
  • Replace if bleeding continues
  • Minor oozing is normal
💊

Medication

  • Take prescribed pain meds
  • Finish all antibiotics
  • Avoid aspirin
🧊

Swelling & Diet

  • Ice packs 15 min on/off
  • Eat cold, soft foods
  • No straws, no hot liquids

🌱 Days 1 – 3: Early Recovery

Keep it simple – focus on gentle care.

🧂

Saline Rinses

  • ½ tsp salt in warm water
  • Rinse 3–4x daily
  • Do not swish hard
🪥

Oral Hygiene

  • Brush gently, avoid surgical site
  • Use soft-bristled brush
  • No flossing near implant
🍲

Diet

  • Semi-soft foods (eggs, soup)
  • Avoid hot/spicy/crunchy
  • Chew on opposite side

📅 Week 1 – 2: Continued Healing

Gradually return to normal, but stay cautious.

🩹

Follow-up

  • Attend suture removal
  • Dentist checks healing
  • Continue saline rinses
🥗

Diet & Hygiene

  • Reintroduce firmer foods slowly
  • Resume full brushing with soft brush
  • Avoid hard/crunchy on implant side
⚠️

Still Avoid

  • Smoking / tobacco
  • Straws
  • Strenuous exercise

🔄 Long-Term Care & Maintenance

Your dental implant can last a lifetime with proper care.

1

Daily Brushing

Brush twice daily with a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste

2

Interdental Cleaning

Use floss, interdental brushes, or water flossers daily around the implant

3

Regular Dental Visits

Schedule professional cleanings and check-ups every 6 months

4

Avoid Harmful Habits

No smoking, no chewing ice/hard objects, no using teeth as tools

5

Wear a Night Guard

If you grind your teeth (bruxism), use a custom night guard

6

Healthy Lifestyle

Maintain a balanced diet rich in calcium and vitamin D

💊

Important: Smokers have a significantly higher risk of implant failure. Consider quitting or reducing during the healing period.

📋 Do's & Don'ts — Quick Reference

Print this page or take a screenshot for easy reference.

✅ DO

  • Apply ice packs for the first 24 hours (15 min on/off)
  • Take all prescribed medications as directed
  • Eat soft, cold foods in the first 24 hours
  • Keep your head elevated when resting
  • Rinse gently with warm salt water after 24 hours
  • Maintain good oral hygiene around the implant
  • Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments
  • Drink plenty of water

❌ DON'T

  • Do NOT use straws for at least 1 week
  • Do NOT smoke or use tobacco products
  • Do NOT consume hot foods/beverages in first 24 hours
  • Do NOT spit forcefully or swish vigorously
  • Do NOT touch the surgical site with fingers or tongue
  • Do NOT engage in strenuous exercise for 48–72 hours
  • Do NOT drink alcohol for at least 72 hours
  • Do NOT brush the surgical site directly until cleared

⚠️ When to Contact Your Dentist Immediately

These symptoms may indicate complications that require prompt professional attention.

🩸
Excessive Bleeding Bleeding that doesn't stop after 4+ hours of gauze pressure
🤒
Fever Temperature above 38°C (100.4°F) lasting more than 24 hours
😣
Severe Pain Pain that worsens after 48 hours or isn't relieved by medication
💛
Pus or Discharge Any yellowish/white discharge or bad taste from the implant site
😮
Implant Feels Loose Any movement or looseness of the implant fixture
😵
Allergic Reaction Rash, hives, difficulty breathing, or facial swelling beyond normal
